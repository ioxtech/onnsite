How To Delete Your Account

We’re sorry to see you go! If you’d like to request the deletion of your account and associated data, please follow the steps below:

Step 1: Send us an Email

To delete your account, please email us at admin@onnsites.com with the subject line:

Subject : Account Deletion Request

In the email, include the following details for verification purposes:

  • Your full name
  • Your registered email address
  • Your username
  • A brief confirmation that you wish to delete your account permanently

    • Step 2: Wait For Confirmation

    Once we receive your request, we will process it within 7 days. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted.

    Important Information

  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Any data associated with your account, including saved preferences, may also be removed.
  • If you change your mind, please contact us before the deletion is finalized.

    • If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to us at admin@onnsites.com.

    Thank you for being part of our community!
